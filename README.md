## Overview

Infrared imaging provides reliable thermal target responses, while polarization imaging contains complementary material-, reflection-, and structure-sensitive information. However, existing infrared--polarization image fusion methods may overemphasize dominant infrared responses, suppressing weak yet informative polarization textures in challenging low-visibility and visually concealed scenes.

We propose **IRPol-Fuse**, an energy--structure coordinated infrared--polarization image fusion framework designed to jointly preserve **infrared thermal saliency** and **polarization-derived structural details**.

IRPol-Fuse consists of three key components:

- **Polarization Attention Fusion (PAF)**  
  Adaptively allocates infrared and polarization information after cross-modal interaction.

- **Infrared Highlight Injector (IHJ)**  
  Preserves thermally salient infrared responses through highlight-guided adaptive infrared reinjection.

- **Polarization Texture Injector (PTJ)**  
  Restores polarization-derived structural details through frequency-domain enhancement, second-order gradients, adaptive contrast enhancement, and multi-scale texture extraction.

In addition, we construct **LI-PI**, a dedicated infrared--polarization dataset for challenging low-visibility and visually concealed scenarios.

The overall framework follows a three-stage pipeline:

1. **Modality-specific feature encoding**
2. **Cross-modal long-range interaction**
3. **Sequential energy--structure coordination through PAF, IHJ, and PTJ**

The preliminary fused representation generated by PAF is first refined by IHJ to preserve thermally salient infrared responses. PTJ subsequently restores polarization-derived texture information to obtain the final fused image.

# LI-PI Dataset

We construct **LI-PI**, a dedicated infrared--polarization dataset for challenging low-visibility and visually concealed scenarios.

LI-PI contains:

- **110** strictly aligned infrared--polarization image pairs
- **90** training pairs
- **10** validation pairs
- **10** independent testing pairs

The dataset contains representative scenes such as:

- Parking garages
- Balconies
- Forests
- Corridors
- Local glare interference
- Visually concealed targets
- Weak-structure and low-visibility environments
